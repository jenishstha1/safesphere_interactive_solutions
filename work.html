Client Case Study · S&S Logistics

SafeShift: Interactive Manual Handling & Ergonomics

How SafeSphere replaced static compliance video sessions with an active 360° warehouse simulation and audit-proof analytics platform.

Project Background

The Client: S&S Logistics operates regional distribution centres handling high-throughput palletised freight, forklift cross-docking, and manual parcel sorting.

The Challenge: Warehouse operatives were reporting elevated rates of lower-back strain and near-miss collisions. Annual mandatory training consisted of 45-minute passive video modules with no real-world decision-making or practical hazard spotting.

The SafeSphere Solution

SafeSphere engineered SafeShift: a multi-role, responsive web application delivering a structured 5-stage training journey:

  1. Learn: Bite-sized interactive ergonomics and HSE guidance covering HSG76 guidelines and manual lifting limits.
  2. Spot & Decide: A 360° navigable warehouse simulation with 4 critical hazards (leaning high stacks, blocked fire escape, bad lifting posture, chemical spill).
  3. Assess: Scored knowledge check evaluating procedure recall under timed exam conditions.
  4. Verify: Live supervisor dashboard recording attempts, scores, and completion certificates.

Key Features Delivered

  • Server-Enforced RBAC: Dedicated views and capabilities for Trainees, Trainers, and System Administrators.
  • Checkpoint Resume: Trainees can pause simulations and resume immediately from their latest verified state.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance: Full keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, screen reader ARIA landmarks, and high-contrast colorways.
  • Permanent Audit Log: Immutable attempt history with timestamps satisfying UK HSE regulatory inspections.
