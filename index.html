Traversing 5 interactive warehouse zones, workers encounter real-life scenarios: unstable pallet stacking, blocked fire escape exits, hazardous lifting postures, and chemical transit spills. Built on Recognized Health & Safety Standards

Every scenario, prompt, and feedback dialogue is mapped to UK statutory health and safety legislation.

HSG76

Warehousing & Storage

Racking load thresholds, aisle clearance, pallet integrity, and overhead falling load mitigation.

PUWER 1998

Work Equipment

Safe operation of lift trucks, reach trucks, daily pre-shift inspections, and mechanical safety guarding.

MHOR 1992

Manual Handling

Ergonomic lifting postures, weight limits, center-of-gravity management, and two-person team lifts.

RRO 2005

Fire Safety Order

Zero tolerance for blocked emergency exit paths, daily escape route audits, and fire door clearance.
