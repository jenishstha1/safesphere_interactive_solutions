Design Language & Identity

Our Brand

Our identity draws on the visual language of SafeShift: a deep slate-to-teal canvas for concentration, cyan for action and navigation, and red reserved solely for safety signals.

The SafeSphere Logo

Symbology: A stylized spherical 'S' ribbon encircled by an orbital ring, symbolising holistic 360° safety coverage and interconnected industrial training solutions.

Clear Space Rule: Maintain generous clear space around the orbital ring equivalent to at least half the sphere's radius. Never crowd or crop the orbital ring.

Color Consistency: The orbital ring preserves a vibrant cyan-to-teal gradient tone. Red is strictly excluded from general branding and used exclusively for active safety warnings.

Colour Palette

High-contrast tones engineered to exceed WCAG 2.1 AA luminance ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy).

  • Canvas #13232a — Deep focus background
  • Teal slate #28495a — Surface cards and panels
  • Action cyan #00e5ff — Interactive links and primary actions
  • Compliance green #22c55e — Verified safe procedures & pass states
  • Safety red #e5484d — Active hazard alerts & critical risk flags
  • Pure white #ffffff — Headings and crisp high-contrast body text

Typography System

Font Family: "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, system-ui, sans-serif

Hierarchy: Bold headings (`font-weight: 700 / 800`), regular body (`400`), crisp code annotations (`font-family: monospace`).

Tone of Voice: Clear, confident, practical, and accountable. Free of corporate jargon; focused entirely on real-world worker safety.
