About SafeSphere

We build interactive training simulations that empower logistics workers to spot hazards, take safe actions, and stay safe on the job.

Who We Are

SafeSphere Interactive Solutions is an enterprise software team (Team 4, CET257 Enterprise Project, University of Sunderland) working to solve real industrial safety challenges.

Our flagship product, SafeShift, was developed in response to a real-world client brief from S&S Logistics. Rather than forcing operatives through passive slide decks, we turn health and safety training into an immersive, browser-based simulation where staff actively explore 360° warehouse bays, identify risks, and make real-time decisions.

Every feature we build is directly traceable to accredited safety guidelines including UK HSE HSG76 (Warehousing and Storage), PUWER 1998, the Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992, and the Fire Safety Order 2005.

Our Mission

To make workplace safety training engaging, practical, and measurable so every employee can confidently recognise risk and make safe decisions before an incident occurs.

Our Vision

A logistics and warehousing industry where hands-on, scenario-based digital learning is the baseline standard for cultivating zero-harm working habits.

Our Values

  • Safety First: In our engineering, UI design, and regulatory advice.
  • Clarity: Plain language, intuitive controls, and honest project scope.
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant design usable across all devices.
  • Evidence-Based: Every feature verified against formal requirements.
