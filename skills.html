HSE Regulatory Alignment

Our simulation logic and procedural scoring reflect verified UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) standards:

HSE HSG76 & PUWER 1998

Warehousing and Storage Safety: Regulates overhead rack clearances, load shrink-wrapping standards, forklift aisle demarcation, and pallet stability limits.

Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992

MHOR Ergonomics Guidance: Governs maximum guideline lifting thresholds (25kg male / 16kg female waist height), mechanical assist requirements, and neutral spinal alignment.

Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005

Emergency Route Clearance: Mandates 100% unobstructed escape paths, push-bar exit verifications, emergency signage luminance, and daily egress inspections.

COSHH & HASAWA 1974

Chemical Spill Response: Defines protocol for identifying unknown liquids, immediate physical barricading, deploying absorbent particulate kits, and PPE compliance.
